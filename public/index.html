About

I'm a third-year Information and Communications Technology (Software Engineering) student at the Singapore Institute of Technology. I like taking an idea from a rough sketch to something people actually use: my projects range from a community platform for Malaysian VTubers and artists to a karaoke queue app my club runs on event nights.

Most of my work is in TypeScript and Next.js, with C# and ASP.NET Core on the back end. I care about clean architecture, testing, and knowing where the time goes when an app gets slow.
